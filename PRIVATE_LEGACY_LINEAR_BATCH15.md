A detached collector reruns the *actual sealed activate* on birth actor/cast facts and gathers one launch declaration without state, RNG, damage or resource access. Whole public onContact substitutions0/35 and definition damage60 work without an original skill fallback. A replaced valid plan MP35/CD2/startup.1 commits once through the native cast path.

Optional packClock.legacyLinear carries rulesHash/round, birth counters/latest source ids, exact origin/cast facts, pending nativeIndex bijection and compact actual flight clock. Restore checks regenerated immutable payload/speed/range/radius/full cast model; mutable position/travel against flight elapsed; reflected owner/direction/anchor/reset; source/cast/native identity and counters. It rebinds to restored objects only after all validation succeeds. Source-declared coefficients compare exactly; accumulated floating movement/clock uses1e-7 tolerance. This is cross-field consistency and one-process callback provenance, not cryptographic authentication of coherently forged whole worlds or malicious hidden factory closure state.
